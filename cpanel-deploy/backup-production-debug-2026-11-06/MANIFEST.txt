Why
---
The live .env has APP_ENV=local and APP_DEBUG=true, so every 500 showed the
full Laravel stack trace (file paths, SQL, bound parameters) to the visitor.
That is how the RouteNotFoundException page was visible in the browser.

What changed
------------
Six lines at the top of bootstrap/app.php: when HTTP_HOST ends with
dreamrootsmilleniumschool.com, APP_DEBUG is set to false and APP_URL to
https://dreamrootsmilleniumschool.com in $_SERVER/$_ENV before Laravel loads
.env. Laravel loads .env immutably, so these values win. Localhost and the
test suite are untouched and keep APP_DEBUG=true.

This is a safety net, not a replacement for fixing .env. The hosting account
should still be corrected to APP_ENV=production, APP_DEBUG=false and
APP_URL=https://dreamrootsmilleniumschool.com.
